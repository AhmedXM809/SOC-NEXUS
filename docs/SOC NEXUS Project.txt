🚀 Introducing SOC NEXUS — My Evolving SOC Automation Center

From separate security tools to one unified workspace. That's the idea behind SOC NEXUS.

I've been building and continuously improving this project to bring the key components of my SOC lab together in one centralized, analyst-focused dashboard.

🔹 The technology behind the project
- Splunk — Security monitoring, detection visibility, and incident data.
- n8n — Workflow automation and execution tracking.
- MISP — Threat intelligence and IOC enrichment.
- TheHive — Incident management and investigation workflows.

🛡️ What SOC NEXUS currently offers
- Centralized security operations overview.
- System health monitoring.
- Detection analytics and incident exploration.
- Investigation and incident visibility.
- Automation history and response pipeline.
- A dedicated audio command center with configurable notifications.
- Authenticated dashboard access and analyst-focused controls.

My philosophy is simple visibility, security, and analyst control.

This is more than a dashboard interface. It is a hands-on engineering project where I bring together security technologies, automation, monitoring, integration, and user experience to build a more organized SOC environment.

And I'm not stopping here.

🚧 SOC NEXUS is an evolving project, not a finished product. I will continue upgrading it, improving its design, strengthening its security, refining its monitoring capabilities, and introducing new features and smarter workflows.

Every improvement is another step toward building a more capable and professional Security Operations Center environment.

The journey is still ongoing — and there is much more to come.

Built, tested, improved, and continuously evolving.
